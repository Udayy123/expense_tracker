--In Django REST Framework (DRF), serializer relations dictate how model relationships (ForeignKey, 
ManyToManyField, and OneToOneField) are represented in your API responses and handled during creation
or updates.By default, a ModelSerializer will represent a relationship field using its Primary Key 
(ID). However, DRF provides several alternative ways to serialize and deserialize relational data
